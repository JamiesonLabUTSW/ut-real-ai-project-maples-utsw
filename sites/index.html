UT-HIP

Participating Institutions

Six UT Medical Schools, One Shared Project

This project, funded by the UT REAL Health AI Pilot Program, brings together six University of Texas health institutions — from the state's largest medical centers to its newest medical school. UT Southwestern serves as the coordinating hub with a production-deployed AI grading system; partner sites are progressing through site-specific onboarding.
